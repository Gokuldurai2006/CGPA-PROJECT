How it works

GPA formula

GPA = Σ(subject credits × grade points) ÷ Σ(subject credits). Grade points and the maximum scale depend on your institution.

CGPA formula

CGPA = Σ(semester GPA × eligible semester credits) ÷ Σ(eligible semester credits).

Data storage

Without Supabase configuration, settings and results are stored in this browser on this device. With Supabase configured, signed-in users can sync semester summaries and profile details to their account.

Exports

Use Analytics & Goals to export a CSV or print/save a PDF report.

Important

Universal mode supports configurable 4-point, 5-point, 10-point, 100-point, and custom maximum scales. You must manually configure official grade-point mappings; automatic conversion between universities is not reliable. Percentage conversion is institution-specific and is not inferred.
